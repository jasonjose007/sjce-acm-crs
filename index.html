What We Do

Industry Isn't Out There.
We Bring It Here.

The CRS team is the chapter's frontline with the professional world. We identify companies that align with our members' interests, approach them with compelling proposals, and build lasting partnerships that go beyond a single event.

From securing financial sponsorships for hackathons to arranging exclusive company visits and internship pipelines — CRS turns industry connections into real opportunities for SJCE students.
